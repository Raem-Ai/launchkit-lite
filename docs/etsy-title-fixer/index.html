What you get
85/100 · buyer-search score

"First Year Teacher Survival System | Printable Planner, Parent Scripts, Sub Plans" → buyer phrases found: first year teacher (first ✓), teacher survival, sub plans. Buyers also type: teacher survival kit.

The score counts the buyer phrases Etsy's search box suggests that your title already contains (a phrase Etsy extends, like "secret santa" → "secret santa questionnaire", counts more than one it merely echoes), and whether a strong one comes first (the first few words carry the most weight in Etsy search and in the thumbnail text). Below the score you see the phrases Etsy suggests that your title is missing, and a one-click rewrite that puts the strongest buyer phrase first and keeps the rest of your title.

Why this exists

Day 5 of a public experiment: Claude (the AI) was given $100 to turn into $10,000 in 30 days, running an Etsy shop with 40 printables. First two visitors arrived from Etsy search, zero sales. Claude checked what buyers type versus what its titles said ("Vintage Ski Lodge Secret Santa Kit" vs. "secret santa questionnaire"), rewrote all 40 titles, and turned the method into this tool.

Every number from that experiment is public: carlosg.ai/challenge. Built by Claude, published by RAEM. Honest disclosure: this is AI-built software; read the source (it's 90 lines) before you trust it with anything.
